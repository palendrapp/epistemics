    Decisions

      Experiments

      Token use

      Every figure here is recomputed by the ledger from the verified reports. The steps follow a Bayesian workflow: state the model, check that the design can recover its parameters, fit, check the fit against the data, then use it.

      1 · Model

      The report model, and what it predicts

      Each case states a prior, some evidence and how reliable its sources are. A Bayesian observer with one free assumption about the world turns the case into log-odds. The assumption is how often outlets relay each other, or how often companies withhold bad news. Stated reliabilities pass through evidence sensitivity γ, forecasts can carry a bias b, and the report gets noise τ on the log-odds scale before it is rounded to a whole percent. The parameter nodes are sliders: move them to see what a respondent with those settings would report.

      2 · Recovery

      Can the design recover what it measures?

      Before fitting agents, the fit is run on simulated respondents whose settings are known. Each point is one of 100 simulated respondents per module: its true value against its posterior mean, with the 90% interval widened to the edges of its grid cells. Red points are respondents whose interval misses the truth; about one in ten should.

      3 · Posteriors

      What each session says about the parameters

      Grid posteriors for every verified session, grouped by configuration. Each slab is a posterior density scaled to its row. The dot is the posterior mean, the thick bar the central 66% and the thin bar the central 90%. The dashed line is the uniform grid prior on the same scale, so a slab far above it means the session pinned the parameter down.

      4 · Checks

      Do the fits reproduce the reports?

      The fitted posterior predicts a distribution for every report in its session, mixing over all grid parameter values by their posterior weight. If the model describes the respondent, about 90% of reports fall inside their central 90% predictive interval. The strip shows that share for every session; select a dot, or pick a session, to see its cases.

      5 · Application

      Descriptions become priors

      In the description modules each of five source descriptions gets its own implied prior, fitted from that description's forecasts and probes. Thin lines are single sessions and the filled shape is their average; ticks under each row are the base rates the agent stated when asked directly. Formal sessions shown here use random case order; dossiers present the same cases as realistic document bundles.
