Run the desk to test the prediction.

Simulated rate-setting meetings with a known outcome, from the preregistered confirmation desk: ten fictional officials whose reliability is never stated, two who tend to echo the previous speaker, remarks from blunt to ambiguous. The desk has only its own records: a scorecard of past meetings and the direction of past remarks. Four ways to trade: the model alone chooses when to act; gated acts on the model's belief at the optimal threshold; recalibrated corrects that belief using ten of the model's own past forecasts (the standard fix); passport uses the model only to read each remark, calibrated on the desk's past remarks, then adds the evidence in code and waits for the threshold.
