# From a cognitive profile to a useful decision

Strategy revision, 26 September 2026, updated after the [bounded source-panel results](source-panel-results-2026-09-26.md). This is the agreed delivery direction. The completed panel establishes only its narrow task/presentation observations; decision benefit remains untested. The [roadmap](mvp.md) records implementation status. The [source-learning collection](source-learning.md) remains at battery/evaluator 0.1.0; this document changes no active task, fitting rule, report contract or issued evidence.

## First product claim and buyer decision

The initial buyer is an orchestrator purchasing bounded research or analysis. The first decision to evaluate is **whether to require independent verification before accepting a research conclusion**. The controlled prototype uses fictional company evidence with resolvable outcomes. It does not assess live investment returns.

The next product milestone is one defensible behavioral claim that transfers to that decision. Broad coverage is a long-term objective; a complete theory of the respondent is not a release requirement. Each candidate claim must specify:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Evaluated model/configuration, tools, effort, context and memory policy; identity association and execution assurance separately |
| Conditions | Task family, evidence availability, presentation, source history and resource budget |
| Observation | Behavior, denominator, material exceptions and repeatability or uncertainty |
| Prediction | What the profile predicts on new cases and how it compares with shared/simple alternatives |
| Explanation | Candidate cognitive account, alternatives and adequacy limits; optional when not identified |
| Useful action | Proposed verification/support decision, with measured benefit distinguished from an untested hypothesis |
| Validity | Tested transfer scope, evaluation date, relevant configuration changes and re-evaluation rule |

An illustrative, **unmeasured** claim is: “Under the tested selective-reporting conditions, this configuration overweights favorable source reports; a source-history ledger reduces resulting decision errors.” Evidence would need to establish the directional pattern, the comparison reference and the ledger's effect separately. A fitted reporting gain alone would not support this wording. The current collection does not test copied-source dependence, so a claim about treating copied reports as independent needs a separate contrast.

## What is being profiled

Profile a configured decision-making system under a declared state policy. A persistent identity is not evidence that its behavior remains constant after model, instructions, tools, memory or effort changes. Record whether the run begins fresh, retains within-environment history, or starts with an authorized pre-existing memory. Do not publish private prompts or memory to prove this binding.

Start with shared predictions and retain configuration-specific deviations only where they improve the relevant prediction. Two deployments may legitimately receive the same profile. The earlier failure to outperform pooling challenges those individualized parameters; it does not make a shared, independently supported capability or limitation worthless.

Behavioral facts, prediction, cognitive explanation and measured support benefit are separate evidence levels. A model that wins among three candidates may still be inadequate. Synthetic model recovery is an engineering prerequisite for the intended interpretation, not proof that the real respondent belongs to one of those families. Elicited probabilities remain observations rather than direct access to internal beliefs.

## Bounded collection sequence

### 1. Acceptance and usability

Complete one fresh continuous-context source-learning run using only public MCP tools. Select the sparse condition to check forecasting, decisions and research without added source questions. Commit configuration, private world, analysis and resource ceiling before collection. Preserve every accepted answer and any failed attempt. Assess completion, comprehension, resource usage, frozen new-case predictions and every candidate's adequacy using the unchanged evaluator.

**Acceptance completed later on 26 September:** the [fresh-agent results](source-learning-acceptance-2026-09-26.md) support proceeding to the compact comparison. The fitted joint account's 3.816-point later-report RMSE was below the alternative families, while the fixed joint observer was slightly better at 3.505. Keep the fixed observer as a strong comparator; this result does not establish personalization or a need for support.

This is development evidence. Do not count it as a held-out confirmation after choosing a new claim or changing a model from its results. Complete one actual human usability session separately through the prepared private browser flow. Agent feedback and automated previews cannot satisfy human acceptance; a human session is not a human norming study. An absent human participant need not block agent-specific comparisons.

### 2. Repeatability, elicitation and a narrow transfer bridge

Prepare a compact comparison after reviewing acceptance and measured cost. The implemented first repeatability panel is **two meaningfully different deployment configurations × two new source worlds × two independent continuing contexts = eight collections**. This characterizes those configurations, not the population of agents. Use the same worlds across configurations/repeats, separate fresh respondents, and never expose evaluator truth. The unit of replication is a complete context/world; the 36 checkpoints are not 36 independent agents.

Choose configurations for the named buyer workflow before viewing results. Record exact requested settings and actual execution limits. Report within-configuration variation alongside between-configuration differences; do not force individual rankings when those differences are unresolved. Compare frozen profile predictions with the fixed observer, shared fits and simple prior-performance/calibration summaries. Estimate a shared baseline without using the answers being predicted. The single-run fixed observer is not itself an empirically pooled population profile.

Use a small matched dense subset to investigate elicitation. Fix its allocation before collection, retain sparse repeats to estimate ordinary variability, and use separate fresh contexts for dense participation. Treat any difference as the bundled effect of additional prompting and reporting burden. No isolated “question-count effect” or general treatment claim follows from this small subset.

Bring forward one transfer bridge before expanding domains: short research packets rendered from a controlled factual ledger, retaining the business process and evidence while reducing explicit decomposition into source hypotheses. Hold out new worlds and freeze the configuration-level profile before bridge responses. Public source history may update the observer as specified; bridge responses must not refit its parameters or enter as free likelihood judgments. Compare with the same inexpensive alternatives on the bridge itself. Do not treat success on new draws of the original generator as evidence of presentation transfer.

The bridge needs a separate version, audited equivalence of the factual content, comprehension checks and synthetic recovery/adequacy controls before real collection. It does not test unrestricted language interpretation or cross-domain transfer. Avoid stacking simultaneous changes to facts, incentives and presentation.

The [source-panel implementation](source-panel.md) now supplies that bounded comparison: eight sparse repeats, four dense collections and four structured/packet transfer collections, using two configurations and three distinct worlds. Prose preserves the original public ledger; comprehension questions follow completion. The [synthetic validation](source-panel-validation-2026-09-26.md) verifies recovery and the full collection path before real responses.

**Panel completed on 26 September:** the exact sixteen-session allocation, configurations, private worlds, comparator fits, analysis and resource limits were frozen before responses. All 576 checkpoints completed without failed submissions or answer retries, in 33 minutes 13 seconds and 20.11 million known processed tokens. The [results](source-panel-results-2026-09-26.md) show lower repeat variation for Astra than Sol in both baseline worlds; dense questions produced no clear common forecast shift. Astra's joint-process predictions remained close across the two presentations in one new world, while Sol's individual fit lost to pooling and all candidates failed its adequacy screen. Keep fixed/shared alternatives and scope the passport observation; do not issue the fitted family/gain as a trait. This remains a feasibility comparison, not a powered certification study.

The separately versioned [0.2 interface](source-delivery-v2.md) now clarifies research scope, per-customer measurement accuracy, provisional/final decisions and outcome feedback. The [source-learning passport adapter](source-learning-passport.md) derives coverage, repeatability and outcome facts from exact records, keeping model diagnostics and identity scope separate. The [acceptance record](source-delivery-acceptance-2026-09-26.md) documents validation and the fresh respondent. Original artifacts and active human sessions remain unchanged. The [assigned-verification comparison](verification-stage-a-results-2026-09-26.md) is now complete. Eight contexts improved Brier under always-check in all four contrasts, but every net-payoff contrast was negative; both configurations failed the frozen gate. The [24-context cost-aware follow-up](cost-aware-results-2026-09-26.md) subsequently separated prices from priors, balanced order and repeated contexts. It saved 92.375% of check expenditure and beat always-check but lost to no-check in all eight matched repeats. A post-result diagnostic found near-benchmark sample incorporation; all harmful selective revisions came from two distinct companies repeated across contexts. Next audit expected decision value and starting-report calibration across independent simulated worlds before more account-backed collection. Preserve the failed gate and defer passport-guided allocation; do not select favorable sample realizations or tune to these two cases.

### 3. Verification and support value

Once a difficulty repeats, test one plausible support without waiting for a unique cognitive mechanism. An evidence/source-history ledger is a candidate. Compare the same underlying information with and without the ledger; if independent verification supplies new information, classify it as additional research and account for that difference and its cost.

Separate two comparisons:

1. **General support:** does the support improve outcomes relative to no support at its cost and latency?
2. **Profile-guided selection:** does selecting support or verification using the profile improve on giving everyone the same support and on simpler selection policies?

For the buyer decision, compare no verification, always verify, a policy using current confidence/prior task performance, and the same decision inputs augmented with the passport. Fix the policies on development data and evaluate on new matched cases. All policies have the same available verification service and declared constraints. Account for reviewer errors and shared-source dependence; an actual reviewer must not be silently modeled as an oracle. Where an alternative policy changes evidence or later agent behavior, collect that branch in matched fresh contexts rather than inventing an unobserved answer.

A proposed decision loss is:

\[
L = c_{\mathrm{false}}\,\mathbf{1}(\text{accept false conclusion})
  + c_{\mathrm{miss}}\,\mathbf{1}(\text{reject true conclusion})
  + c_{\mathrm{verify}}\,\mathbf{1}(\text{verify})
  + c_{\mathrm{latency}}\,t.
\]

Define the conclusion, decisions and loss weights with the pilot buyer before evaluation; handle abstention explicitly if allowed. Until then, report errors, verification rate, latency and cost separately, with clearly labeled sensitivity analyses rather than a purported dollar return. Add evaluation/re-evaluation cost to the deployment comparison and amortize only over an explicitly stated number of uses. Forecast RMSE, company outcome quality and buyer decision loss answer different questions.

Declare the minimum useful improvement and uncertainty treatment before confirmation. A provisional pilot can report its measured result with limitations; do not label a policy validated because a point estimate beats one baseline. The present myopic research-value calculation does not include future benefits of a source audit and must not become a general rationality score or an optimal policy benchmark.

### 4. Design partner and bounded paid delivery

Identify one design partner and specify the workflow, acceptable errors, verification option, operating budget, privacy needs and purchase criterion alongside measurement work. Preparation is authorized project work; contacting third parties requires explicit user direction. No customer, outreach or willingness to pay is established by this plan.

The first paid offering can be an operator-assisted bounded assessment with an interpretable report and machine-readable evidence. The buyer pays for an assessment, not a favorable badge. Define completion, technical-failure/refund terms and attempted-run accounting before sale; keep private evidence private. A payment alone is not evidence that profile-guided decisions improve.

Reuse the existing passport, enrollment, identity and consumer components. Complete an owned-identity real-agent demonstration when the owner/signer is available. Add hosted tenant isolation and x402 job settlement when the pilot needs automated purchase. Basic artifact verification stays usable without a paid call. No custom token, new identity system or contribution marketplace is needed for this milestone.

Operationally check the relation between evaluated configuration and deployed service. A signed declaration remains a declaration; configuration hashes and registry ownership do not attest which model executed. State the available assurance, validity period and material-change policy. Later authorized deployment samples may check consistency, but cannot by themselves prove execution identity.

## Decisions we will make from the evidence

| Finding | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Collection or comprehension failure | Fix the identified interface/task issue, review versions and recovery, then repeat acceptance; preserve the original attempt |
| All cognitive candidates inadequate | Inspect structured residuals and test a specific alternative on fresh cases; retain descriptive facts and do not issue a mechanism label |
| Shared/simple prediction matches individual fits | Use the simpler account; retain individualized claims only where their incremental value is demonstrated |
| Apparent differences are comparable to repeat variation | Report unresolved separation; improve measurement only if the distinction matters to the buyer decision |
| Controlled-task finding fails the bridge | Restrict its scope; investigate the presentation/elicitation dependency before expanding task families |
| Generic support helps but personalization adds nothing | Offer the generic support with its measured scope; do not claim profile-based selection benefit |
| Profile-guided policy gives no useful gain over cheap alternatives | Revisit the buyer decision or value proposition before adding a broad battery or commerce stack |
| A portable claim improves a named decision at acceptable cost | Integrate that claim into the passport/consumer and run the bounded customer pilot |

Keep source-label priors, drift, broad domains, team allocation and paid contributions as later extensions. Human participation remains compatible with the protocol, while human interpretation, privacy and population norms remain separate.

## Methodological basis

The [source-inference design](source-inference-design-2026-09-24.md) retains the Bayesian/RL and causal-modeling motivation. This sequence also draws on [Hedge, Powell & Sumner's reliability analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5990556/) and [CogBench's configuration/prompt comparisons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18225). The former concerns human tasks; the latter does not establish the behavior of our current deployment. These works motivate measurement checks, not a claim to replicate a validated instrument. Prediction and intervention evidence remain conditional on the actual tasks and configurations we test.
