# Fresh-agent source-learning acceptance — 26 September 2026

One fresh GPT-6 Astra process completed **24 companies / 36 checkpoints** in **3 minutes 16 seconds**, using the sparse public MCP interface in one continuing context. No answer was rejected, changed or retried. The model that represents both source measurement accuracy and selective reporting predicted the later reports substantially more closely than the flat-accuracy and fixed-discount candidates. Fitting an individual reporting gain did not improve average prediction over the fixed joint observer.

This is one configuration in one generated source environment. It supports proceeding to the [small repeatability and transfer comparison](decision-value-pilot.md), not a stable cognitive trait, unique internal mechanism, measured intervention benefit or general passport certification. Actual human usability remains untested.

The calibration comparison concentrated its conditional family weight on the joint-process account. Its best reporting gain was 0.95, with uncertainty retained across the gain grid. That gain is a nuisance parameter under the specified report-noise model; it is not evidence of a general underconfidence or slow-updating trait. Near-unit conditional family weight is relative to these three candidates and their assumptions, not certainty about the respondent's internal computation.

The joint-process family's 3.816-point error was below its prespecified conditional simulation boundary of 4.514 points. The flat-accuracy and fixed-discount candidates were above their respective 4.940- and 5.050-point boundaries. Thus the implementation's screen did not flag the joint account for excessive prediction error. These finite-simulation screens are not calibrated composite-model tests and do not establish that every feature of the response distribution is correct.

The useful structural finding is that accounting for selective reporting as well as measurement accuracy describes this run better than the tested alternatives. The individual fit was **0.311 points worse** than the fixed joint observer. This run does not establish incremental personalization value; neither does it compare against an empirically pooled cohort profile.

The largest residual was 10.119 points for one company, repeated at its initial and unchanged final forecast. The 24 held-out reports concern twelve companies, with six recurring sources; repeated checkpoints must not be counted as independent participants. This single run contains no repeatability or transfer test.
